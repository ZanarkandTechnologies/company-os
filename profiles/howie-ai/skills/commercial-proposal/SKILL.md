# Commercial proposal

Use only after the target, offer, scope, commercial assumptions, and approver
are explicit. Create a proposal draft with clear assumptions, exclusions,
timeline, and approval status using [the template](templates/commercial-proposal.md).
Do not fabricate prices, promises, legal terms, or delivery capacity.

Any missing commercial assumption is a requirement on the ticket, not prose
hidden in the document.
