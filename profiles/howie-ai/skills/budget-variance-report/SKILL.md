# Budget variance report

Use when the manager needs a period variance view. Require the period,
approved budget, actuals source, accountable owner for each material variance,
and a finance reviewer. Do not calculate from absent inputs or describe a
variance as favourable/unfavourable without the approved comparison basis.

Write the artifact from [the template](templates/budget-variance-report.md) to
the owning ticket, cite sources, record drafting events, and request finance
review. Missing actuals or owner explanations create scoped requirements rather
than guessed explanations.
