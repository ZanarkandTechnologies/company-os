# Operating risk brief

Use for one defined operating decision or risk review. Require verified
observations, a risk owner, due date, proposed mitigation, escalation owner,
and reviewer. Use [the template](templates/operating-risk-brief.md). Do not
label a risk controlled, compliant, or acceptable without the cited evidence
and review.

Each mitigation remains a ticketed action; this brief is the evidence-backed
decision surface, not the task board.
