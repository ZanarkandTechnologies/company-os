# Ads campaign

Use for a proposed paid campaign, never a live media buy. Require approved
audience research, offer, budget guardrail, channels, measurement owner, and
reviewer. Write [the campaign brief](templates/ads-campaign-brief.md) in the
ticket artifact directory. Clearly label estimates and prohibit spending,
account access, creative publication, or audience activation.

Block when the offer, budget, or measurement definition is missing.
