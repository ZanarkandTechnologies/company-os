# Contractor procurement pack

Use for a contractor or supplier decision. Require an approved need, scope,
evaluation criteria, source-backed candidate/bid data, budget owner, and
reviewer. Populate [the procurement pack](templates/contractor-procurement-pack.md).
Do not invite, select, contract, or negotiate with any party; the output is a
review draft. Missing bids or criteria become ticket requirements.
