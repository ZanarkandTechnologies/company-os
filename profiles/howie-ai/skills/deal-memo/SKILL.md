# Deal memo

Use for a specific deal, partnership, or investment decision. Require a named
counterparty, the decision to make, verified economics/technical evidence, and
a named reviewer. Distinguish facts, open diligence points, assumptions, and
recommendations. Do not create legal, investment, or binding commitments.

Use [the template](templates/deal-memo.md), save it under the owning ticket,
and record citations, unresolved diligence, and review state.
