# Customer research

Use for a defined customer, investor, partner, or market audience question.
Require the target segment, research objective, verified source set, and a
named reviewer. Separate observed facts from hypotheses and do not present an
example document as current market evidence.

Create the draft using [the template](templates/customer-research-brief.md),
with citations for every factual claim. Record source provenance and review
state on the ticket. The resulting approved brief may feed outreach, ads,
commercial proposals, or fundraising work.
