# Outreach campaign

Use after an approved audience research brief and explicit offer/call-to-action
direction are available. Produce a reviewable targeting rationale, sequence,
and measurement plan from [the template](templates/outreach-campaign.md).
Never send messages, scrape contacts, or claim consent. The campaign is a
draft until a named growth reviewer approves it.

If audience research or offer direction is missing, keep the ticket blocked
with a scoped human or upstream-output requirement.
