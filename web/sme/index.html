01 / Durable systems

Three systems.
One accountable operating layer.

An agent can read, propose, and execute within a bounded route. It never becomes the unowned place where the company remembers what happened.

01 / Operational System owned

Connected applications

People keep updating the tools they already use. Agents help through reviewed integrations; the application remains authoritative.

Owns
Transactions, records, and operational state
Updated by
Humans or bounded integration actions
Does not own
Company-wide facts or decision rationale
02 / Knowledge Queryable

Company wiki

A separate knowledge workflow checks and dreams sourced facts into an LLM-ready wiki that people and agents query when needed.

Owns
Facts, definitions, sources, and freshness
Updated by
Knowledge ingestion and review
Does not own
Approval authority or process execution
03 / Governance Approval bound

Decision ledger

Decisions stay separate so context, options, rationale, scope, approval, responsibility, dates, and supersession remain auditable.

Owns
Decisions, accountability, and review state
Updated by
Named decision owners and approvers
Does not own
Raw facts or application transactions

03 / Procedure compiler

become portable skill.

A skill is a compiled operating procedure—not remembered instinct. It points back to the decision that authorized it and carries explicit inputs, outputs, guardrails, approvals, and evaluations.

05 / Core principle

No agent memory
required.

The runtime can be replaced tomorrow. The company still knows what is true, who decided, who is responsible, which procedure is active, and what changed in the applications.

  • 01Agents are replaceableContext is retrieved for the task.
  • 02Systems are durableState has a named owner and update path.
  • 03Approvals are explicitChat tone never substitutes for authority.
  • 04Skills are portableProcedure versions outlive any one model.
